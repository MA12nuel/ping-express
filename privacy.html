Privacy policy for Ping Express

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Short version

Ping Express does not collect, send or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What is stored on your device

The game saves your settings (sound, vibration, difficulty), whether you finished the tutorial, and your current match in your browser's local storage on your own device, so you can continue later. This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the site or app data.

Network requests

When online, the game loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Google may receive your IP address for that request, as described in Google's privacy policy. The game works without these fonts.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
